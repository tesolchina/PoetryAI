## SECTION 4: Interaction Types and Preferences
**Research Question 4: Interaction type ranking**

### Understanding Interaction Types

During your session, the AI interacted with you in different ways. We've identified three main types:

**Type A - Constraint Repair (Diagnostic/Corrective)**
- The AI identifies technical issues (grammar, structure, syllable count) and helps you fix them
- Example: "This line has 8 syllables but needs 7 for the haiku structure. Let's adjust it."

**Type B - Content Enhancement (Example-based)**
- The AI provides multiple options or examples for you to choose from
- Example: "Here are three possible opening lines: 1) ... 2) ... 3) ... Which resonates with you?"

**Type C - Surprise Harvest (Exploratory)**
- The AI offers unexpected ideas or directions you hadn't considered
- Example: "What if we explored this from the perspective of...?" or "I'm noticing a metaphor emerging here..."
