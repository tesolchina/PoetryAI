## Executive Summary

The second-round feedback reveals **highly positive reception** of the PoetryAI platform. All seven participants gave perfect or near-perfect satisfaction ratings, with strong intentions to use the platform in the future. However, data indicates varying levels of creative autonomy and specific areas for improvement in AI interaction features.

## 9. Critical Issues & Recommendations

### Issue #1: Type A (Constraint Repair) Functionality
**Problem**: 43% of participants found Type A interactions most frustrating  
**Example**: Format fixing resulted in longer, not shorter, output  
**Recommendation**: 
- Review constraint-fixing algorithm
- Add user confirmation before applying fixes
- Provide explanation of changes made

### Issue #2: Word Choice & Language Quality
**Problem**: 57% disagreed with AI-suggested word choices  
**Recommendation**:
- Implement alternative word suggestion sets
- Allow users to rate word quality and train on feedback
- Consider dialect/register options (formal, casual, poetic)

### Issue #3: Emotional Authenticity
**Problem**: 2 participants disagreed with emotional aspects  
**Recommendation**:
- Add emotional tone/sentiment controls
- Provide user guidance on personalizing emotional content
- Consider pre-writing emotion specification feature

### Issue #4: Data Collection Issues
**Problem**: Some survey responses are unclear or missing  
**Recommendation**:
- Clarify multi-choice vs. open-ended questions
- Ensure all participants answer all questions
- Pre-validate responses before submission

### Issue #5: The Outlier Case
**Problem**: Zhu Ying Lam gave 5/5 ratings but indicated unlikely future use  
**Recommendation**:
- Follow-up interview to understand disconnect
- May indicate difference between one-time experience satisfaction vs. sustained utility
- Consider longitudinal tracking

## 11. Conclusions

### Strengths:
✅ Exceptional user satisfaction (near-universal 5/5 ratings)  
✅ Strong intention for future use and learning  
✅ Effective as brainstorming and exploration tool  
✅ Platform design praised for clarity and aesthetics  
✅ Image generation feature well-received  
✅ Successfully supports creative expression  

### Areas for Improvement:
⚠️ Constraint repair functionality needs refinement  
⚠️ Language/word choice suggestions require quality enhancement  
⚠️ Emotional authenticity consistency  
⚠️ Data collection methodology clarification  
⚠️ Understanding motivation gaps between satisfaction and actual use  

### Overall Assessment:
**The PoetryAI platform demonstrates strong market-readiness for educational use with excellent user satisfaction.** The system effectively serves its primary purposes of brainstorming, technique learning, and creative exploration. Refinements to AI suggestion quality and constraint handling would elevate the platform to address remaining user concerns.
