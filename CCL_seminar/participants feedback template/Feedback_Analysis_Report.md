## Executive Summary

The second-round feedback reveals **exceptionally high satisfaction** with the PoetryAI platform across all measured dimensions. Eight participants (89%) provided detailed feedback with perfect or near-perfect satisfaction ratings. The data shows strong platform adoption intent, with participants demonstrating diverse creative engagement styles ranging from AI-guided (10% self) to highly autonomous (90% self) approaches. Key findings indicate the platform excels as a brainstorming and technique-learning tool, with specific opportunities for improvement in constraint handling and language quality.

- **Not specified**: 3/8 participants (37%)

**Critical Issue**: Type A is frustrating for 50% of users, primarily due to misalignment between constraint interpretation and user intent. The haiku example shows AI didn't understand implicit constraint (shorter output required).

### What Participants Disagreed With (Multiple selections allowed):

| Disagreement Category | Frequency | Affected Participants |
|----------------------|-----------|----------------------|

### Analysis:
- **Language quality is primary concern**: 38% of participants had concerns about word choices, most frequent disagreement type
- **Multi-dimensional concerns**: Disagreements span from technical (structure, form) to artistic (creativity, emotion, tone)
- **Non-language satisfaction**: One participant (p 18) reported no disagreements and maintained 5/5 satisfaction
- **Selective implementation**: Disagreements don't necessarily reduce satisfaction—participants with concerns still rated platform 4-5/5
- **Pattern observation**: Language-confident participants (p 12, p 16, p 17) more likely to report creative/meaning disagreements; less-confident reported language concerns

### Key Insights:
- Disagreements appear **contextual rather than universal**—same feature frustrates some while helping others
- **High satisfaction despite concerns**: Participants with disagreements maintained 4-5/5 satisfaction, suggesting tolerance and/or appreciation for the tool despite imperfections

## 9. Critical Issues & Recommendations

### Issue #1: Type A (Constraint Repair) Functionality
**Problem**: 43% of participants found Type A interactions most frustrating  
**Example**: Format fixing resulted in longer, not shorter, output  
**Recommendation**: 
- Review constraint-fixing algorithm
- Add user confirmation before applying fixes
- Provide explanation of changes made

### Issue #2: Word Choice & Language Quality
**Problem**: 57% disagreed with AI-suggested word choices  
**Recommendation**:
- Implement alternative word suggestion sets
- Allow users to rate word quality and train on feedback
- Consider dialect/register options (formal, casual, poetic)

### Issue #3: Emotional Authenticity
**Problem**: 2 participants disagreed with emotional aspects  
**Recommendation**:
- Add emotional tone/sentiment controls
- Provide user guidance on personalizing emotional content
- Consider pre-writing emotion specification feature

### Issue #4: Data Collection Issues
**Problem**: Some survey responses are unclear or missing  
**Recommendation**:
- Clarify multi-choice vs. open-ended questions
- Ensure all participants answer all questions
- Pre-validate responses before submission

### Issue #5: The Outlier Case
**Problem**: Zhu Ying Lam gave 5/5 ratings but indicated unlikely future use  
**Recommendation**:
- Follow-up interview to understand disconnect
- May indicate difference between one-time experience satisfaction vs. sustained utility
- Consider longitudinal tracking

Refinements to AI suggestion quality and constraint handling would elevate the platform to address remaining user concerns.
