## Final Free Verse Poem: "Echoes of Forgotten Joy"

```
In the shadow of old buildings,
we ran, carefree and bold,
believing in endless tomorrows,
where happiness never grows old.

We laughed at the smallest wonders,
never burdened by life's weight,
trusting in the magic of our days,
where dreams never seemed too late.

Now, as I stand alone,
the echoes of laughter fade,
those carefree days now shadows,
in the silence where memories are laid.

The old buildings whisper stories,
of a time when joy was pure,
yet now, their silent presence
reminds me of what cannot endure.

Time marches on relentless,
leaving footprints in the dust,
those days of innocence vanished,
as memories turn to rust.

Yet in the heart's quiet corners,
those moments forever reside,
a bittersweet reminder
of the joy time cannot divide.
```

**Title:** Echoes of Forgotten Joy
