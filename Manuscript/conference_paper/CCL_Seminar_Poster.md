## About This Workshop

Discover how **AI parameter configuration transforms creative writing pedagogy**. This hands-on seminar introduces educators to **poetry.aitutor.ink**, a specialized platform designed for L2 poetry writing that puts pedagogical control in teachers' hands.

**Learn how temperature and top-p settings shape student experiences:**

- 🔹 **Low parameters (0.3/0.4)**: Structured guidance, pattern reinforcement, confidence building
- 🔹 **High parameters (0.8/0.9)**: Creative exploration, surprising discoveries, authorship agency
- 🔹 **Strategic configuration**: Match AI behavior to learning goals and student readiness

## Workshop Structure

### Part 1: Introduction (30 min)
- Research findings: How parameters affect authorship perception
- The "helpful but alienating" paradox explained
- Live demonstration of the platform

### Part 2: Hands-On Practice (60 min)
- Participants write poetry with different parameter settings
- Experience Type A, B, and C interactions firsthand
- Reflect on authorship perception across conditions

### Part 3: Pedagogical Applications (30 min)
- Designing progressive curricula (scaffolding fading)
- Troubleshooting common challenges
- Q&A and discussion

---

## Key Research Findings

📊 **7× more creative surprises** with exploratory parameters (35% vs. 5%)  
📊 **6× higher authorship perception** in high-temperature conditions (62.5% vs. 10%)  
📊 **75% prefer "helpful" interactions**, yet they reduce authorship feelings  

> *"It felt like a very good friend, not just a machine"* — Student using exploratory parameters

> *"I only own the meaning, not the making"* — Student with structured parameters

## About the Facilitator

**Yu Ruobin (余若彬)**  
PhD Candidate, Department of English  
Hong Kong Baptist University

**Research Focus**: AI-assisted L2 creative writing, Data-Driven Learning evolution in the generative AI era, parameter-based pedagogy, and creative agency in human-AI collaboration

**Bio**: Ruobin is a doctoral researcher investigating how generative AI can be configured as adaptive pedagogical scaffold rather than fixed intervention in L2 writing contexts. Her research bridges corpus linguistics, educational technology, and creative writing pedagogy, with particular focus on making AI's technical parameters accessible to educators as pedagogical design levers. She has designed and implemented **poetry.aitutor.ink**, a specialized platform enabling parameter-aware L2 poetry instruction. Her current work examines how temperature and top-p settings systematically shape interaction patterns, authorship perception, and creative satisfaction in AI-assisted writing.
