## Slide 5: Change #3 - Poetry vs. Machine Logic

### Challenging Heidegger's Binary

**Original Issue (Dr. Checketts):**  
"Heidegger's Holderland frames poetry as opposite of machine logic. Justify why poetry writing needs or benefits from machine influence."

**Theoretical Dialectic:**

| **Heidegger (1984)** | **Strehovec (2023)** |
|----------------------|----------------------|
| Poetry = purest human expression | Artistic creation = cognitive activity |
| Incompatible with machine logic | Poetry composition = problem-solving |
| Science vs. art binary | Writing = methodology-supported procedure |

**Our Position:**  
"Machine influence is **pedagogically valid when bounded and transparent**: AI provides pattern data and options, while humans retain selection, rejection, and revision authority."

**Specific Justification:**  
"For **beginners in writing**, who have ideas and affective goals but lack linguistic resources, AI-assisted poetry can be DDL when the process foregrounds **noticing, testing, and reflective decision making**."

## Slide 6: Change #4 - DDL Justification

### AI as a New DDL Interface

**Original Issue (Dr. Wang):**  
"Justify AI-assisted learning as DDL and explain why poetry writing is a suitable research target"

**Core Argument:**

**Traditional DDL:**  
Learner → Query → Corpus → Concordance → Discovery

**AI-Assisted DDL:**  
Learner + AI → Pattern Generation → Noticing/Analysis → Selection/Revision

**Key Statement:**  
"AI can serve as a **new DDL interface** if learners treat its outputs as **linguistic data to notice, analyze, and revise**."

**Structural Similarity:**  
- Both involve **data-driven decision making**
- Both require **hypothesis formation and testing**
- Different interfaces, **same learning logic**

**Why Poetry?**  
Traditional DDL shows "what has been written"; AI-assisted DDL enables "**what could be written**" → critical for creative writing where **novelty and surprise are pedagogical goals**

## Slide 7: Parameter → Interaction → Perception Mechanism

### The Technical-Pedagogical Bridge

**The Mechanism We Discovered:**

```
Parameter Configuration
    ↓
Interaction Type Distribution
    ↓
Authorship & Agency Perception
```

**Empirical Evidence:**

| Parameter Condition | Type C (Surprise Harvest) | Authorship Claims |
|---------------------|---------------------------|-------------------|
| **Structured** (T=0.3, top-p=0.4) | 5% | Lower (40-60%) |

**7-fold increase in Type C** (χ²=24.3, p<.001, Cramer's V=.38)

**Key Insight:**  
Parameter manipulation functions as **learner-intuitive pedagogical lever** that educators can adjust to adapt scaffolding

## Slide 8: Three Interaction Types Framework

### DDL-Scaffolding Modes

| Type | Function | Parameter | Scaffolding | Creativity Mode |
|------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **A: Constraint Repair** | Pattern reinforcement | Low | Heavy | Convergent |
| **B: Exemplar Giving** | Model selection | Medium | Moderate | Hybrid |
| **C: Surprise Harvest** | Serendipitous discovery | High | Light | **Divergent** |

**Type A:** "AI as tutor" → corrective guidance  
**Type B:** "AI as resource bank" → option selection  
**Type C:** "AI as creative partner" → collaborative co-creation

## Slide 9: The Type B Paradox

### Helpful but Alienating

**Empirical Finding:**

✓ **Most common** (61.54% voted)  
✓ **Most helpful** (55.56% voted)  
✗ **Reduced authorship perception**  
✗ **Lower creative satisfaction**

**Why Helpful?**  
1. Confidence booster  
2. Convenient tool  
3. Certainty creator  
4. Vocabulary support  
5. Knowledge gap-filler

**Why Alienating?**  
- P02: "I do not feel ownership or pride over much of it"  
- P05: "Feels like just AI... I couldn't feel any sort of creativity"  
- P06: "The poem became more formulaic"

**Cognitive Shift:**  
From **"How do I create?"** to **"Which option do I choose?"**  
Generation → Evaluation | Synthesis → Selection

## Slide 10: Change #5 - Creativity Framework in Discussion

### Type C: Convergent → Divergent Transformation

**Original Issue (Dr. Checketts, Dr. Hung):**  
"Build creativity framework using Guilford's divergent and convergent creativity"

**Our Framework Integration:**

**Type C Mechanism:**
1. **AI generates unexpected suggestions** (divergent input)
2. **Human exercises critical judgment** (evaluation)
3. **Iterative reciprocity emerges** (negotiation cycles)
4. **Authorship activated through rejection/refinement** (agency)

**Theoretical Grounding:**
- Coenen et al. (2022): Serendipitous discovery in human-AI collaboration
- Boden (2004): Divergent thinking and novelty in creative processes
- Qian et al. (2023): Cognitive load redistribution

**Evidence:**  
- P09: 80% authorship through 5 iterative cycles, "Turnstile Justice" poem
- P12: 90% authorship (highest across 30 participants) through critical engagement
- P16: 50-50 co-creation with maximum satisfaction (5/5)

**Core Insight:**  
Type C doesn't just provide patterns—it **provokes divergent thinking while demanding convergent judgment**, creating dialectical creativity that preserves human agency

## Slide 11: Type C Case Evidence

### P12: 90% Authorship Despite 15-20% AI Contribution

**Five Iterative Cycles:**

1. **Initial vision:** "Cursed princess in dragon form" (human)
2. **AI attempt:** Opening lines about transformation
3. **Critical rejection:** "I don't want repetitive use of 'whisper'" (human judgment)
4. **AI refinement:** Adjusted to "hidden hope"
5. **Narrative correction:** "Princess seeks witch's help, not vice versa" (human agency)

**Verbs Matter:**  
P16: "I **asked**, I **instructed**, I **guided**"  
→ Language of **choreography**, not consumption

**Authorship preserved through:**
- Conceptual control
- Critical evaluation
- Selective rejection
- Iterative refinement

## Slide 12: Change #6 - Limitations Acknowledged

### What This Study Did Not Do

**Explicitly Stated Limitations:**

1. **No L1 Comparison Group**  
   - All 30 participants were L2 English learners
   - Cannot isolate language background effects from novice writer effects
   - Risk of conflating L2 challenges with novice challenges

2. **No Long-Period Observation**  
   - Single 75-minute session per participant
   - Cannot track development over time
   - Cannot assess retention or transfer effects

3. **No No-AI Control Condition**  
   - All participants used AI
   - Cannot isolate pure AI effects from other factors
   - Cannot compare AI-assisted vs. unassisted poetry writing

4. **Sample Size (N=30)**  
   - Depth over breadth approach
   - Rich qualitative data but limited generalizability
   - Three phases provide triangulation but not large-scale validation

## Slide 15: Theoretical Contribution Summary

### What This Revised Study Argues

**Core Claim:**  
Parameter manipulation is a **pedagogical design lever** that educators can use to dynamically adapt DDL scaffolding in AI-assisted creative writing

**Three Mechanisms Revealed:**

1. **Parameter → Interaction**  
   Technical settings systematically shape which interaction types emerge

2. **Interaction → Perception**  
   Different interaction types produce distinct authorship and agency experiences

3. **Perception → Pedagogy**  
   Learners intuitively detect scaffolding differences and strategically select modes

**Theoretical Integration:**
- DDL principles (Johns, Boulton, Cobb)
- Scaffolding theory (Vygotsky, Wood et al.)
- Human-AI co-creativity (Coenen, Qian, Li)
- Divergent/convergent creativity (Boden, Guilford)

**Practical Implication:**  
Educators don't need to code—they need to **understand parameter effects and adjust settings** to match learner needs and creative goals

## Slide 18: Closing Statement

### DDL's Evolution, Not Death

**The Original Challenge (Crosthwaite & Baisa 2023):**  
"Generative AI represents the **death of Data-driven Learning**"

**Our Counter-Claim:**  
Generative AI represents DDL's **evolution from static to dynamic**

**From:**  
- Learner-initiated queries → System-initiated pattern presentation
- Fixed corpus evidence → Generative possibilities
- Manual concordance search → Conversational scaffolding
- One-size-fits-all → Parameter-adjustable adaptivity

**The Future of DDL:**  
Not dependent on AI capabilities, but on **educators' capacity to exercise pedagogical judgment over AI configuration**
