## Executive Summary

The PoetryAI Platform Promotion & Publication Plan integrates service learning, research advancement, and creative publication into a cohesive three-phase expansion strategy. Beginning with HKBU as an anchor institution in Q1 2026, the initiative scales to other Hong Kong universities by Q3, then extends to mainland institutions by Q4 and beyond. This dual-purpose model leverages authentic participant engagement to simultaneously support L2 poetry writing development, collect rigorous research data on AI-assisted language learning, and produce a bilingual anthology of 300–500 participant poems. **Expected outcomes:** established institutional partnerships, validated pedagogical protocols, a professionally published anthology distributed to 50+ libraries, and peer-reviewed research publications advancing the field of AI in language education.

## Part 5: Critical Ethical & Design Considerations

### 1. Emotional Safety in Publication

**Challenge:** Participants may share trauma/sensitive emotions in poems

**Protocol:**
- ✅ Document all emotional content in poems during collection
- ✅ Create review process where participants see poems before publication
- ✅ Offer options: publish as-is, anonymize, exclude, rewrite with support
- ✅ Provide mental health resources if distress emerges
- ✅ Never publish without explicit individual consent

**Consent Addition:**
```
I understand my poem may be published in an anthology. I give permission for:
☐ Publication with my name
☐ Publication with first name only
☐ Publication anonymously (with theme only)
☐ Not publishing my poem
☐ Reviewing my poem before publication decision
```

### 2. Selection Bias Prevention

**Challenge:** Temptation to publish only "good" or "impressive" poems

**Safeguard:**
- ✅ Establish selection criteria in advance (transparent)
- ✅ Include diversity of themes, lengths, proficiency levels
- ✅ Consider representative sampling, not curated "best"
- ✅ Document exclusion criteria (e.g., only excluded for safety reasons)
- ✅ Report on selection process transparently

### 3. Privacy at Scale

**Challenge:** As participant pool grows, maintaining anonymity becomes harder

**Solutions:**
- ✅ Separate research data from publication materials early
- ✅ Use anonymization codes (P001, P002) in analysis
- ✅ Store consent separately from content
- ✅ For Mainland participants: consider data localization requirements

### 4. Cross-Border IP & Legal Considerations

**For Mainland participants:**
- ✅ Verify data sharing agreements comply with local regulations
- ✅ Clarify copyright ownership (participant retains rights)
- ✅ Ensure publication complies with local IP laws
- ✅ Consider where server/data is stored

### 5. Dual-Purpose Clarity

**Messaging requirement:**
- ✅ All recruitment materials must clearly state both research AND publication plans
- ✅ No deception about data use
- ✅ Participant autonomy: can participate in research WITHOUT publication consent
- ✅ Can change mind about publication post-hoc

## Part 8: Potential Challenges & Mitigation

| Challenge | Risk | Mitigation Strategy |
|-----------|------|-------------------|
| **Consent complexity** | Participants confused about research vs. publication | Clear separate forms; multiple communication touchpoints |
| **Privacy concerns** | Worry about anonymity with published work | Offer multiple attribution options; storage security |
| **Selection bias** | Only publish "good" poems; skew findings | Pre-establish selection criteria; transparent process; document exclusions |
| **Emotional safety** | Regret about sharing sensitive content | Review process; opt-out option; mental health resources |
| **Timeline pressure** | Publication deadline conflicts with research | Stagger publication (multiple volumes); don't rush final analysis |
| **Institutional politics** | Other universities hesitant to partner | Start with warm contacts; offer tangible benefits; co-authorship |
| **Mainland data issues** | Regulatory compliance concerns | Early consultation with legal/compliance; transparent data handling |
| **Resource constraints** | Publication production is expensive | Explore open access options; partner with publishers; crowdfund if needed |
| **Participant dropout** | Low engagement across phases | Build community; share progress; celebrate participant wins |
